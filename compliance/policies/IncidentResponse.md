Incident Response Plan (Draft)

Purpose
Provide a structured response to security incidents impacting systems in scope.

Policy
- Define incident severity levels and escalation paths.
- Triage and contain incidents promptly; involve legal/PR for high-impact incidents.
- Preserve evidence and avoid destructive actions during investigations.
- Root cause analysis and post-incident report with remediation.

Evidence
- Incident tickets and timelines.
- Communication logs and meeting notes.
- Forensics evidence snapshots (where applicable) stored securely.

Responsibilities
- Incident Response Lead (Security/Ops): coordinate response.
- Engineering: provide technical remediation and root cause analysis.
- Legal/Compliance: advise on breach disclosure and regulatory requirements.
